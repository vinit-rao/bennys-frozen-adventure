# Benny's Frozen Adventure (Made by Brain Freeze)
- [Click to Play Now](https://vinitrao.itch.io/benny-alpha)
- [Watch the Trailer](https://youtu.be/_w7nBbe73mw)
- Benny's ice cream machine has broken and it's your job to catch the scoops and fulfill the orders in our demo!
- Welcome to **Benny's Frozen Adventure**! This is an interactive, grid-based arcade game developed in Unity where you have to help Benny catch scoops, stack massive cones, and fulfill ice cream orders.

## Pitched Ideas vs Final Game
- We had envisioned a lot more for our game especially during the pitch day, however, since we wanted our game to feel as polished as posible, to maintain that goal we needed to leave out certain interactions and mechanics that were mentioned in our pitch presentation. These include...
- Stun Benny if ice cream hits his head.
- Customers appear in the 3D space (still technically met just used a render texture to show them in the ui)
- 5 Levels (droppped down to 3)
- Benny can go to a customer to hand the ice cream (switched to auto complete)
